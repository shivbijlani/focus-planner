Privacy Policy

Focus Planner (plannermd.com) · Effective 30 September 2026

Focus Planner is a planner that keeps your tasks and journals as plain markdown files. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no Focus Planner server, account, or database: we never receive, see, or store your planner data.

Where your data lives

  • In your browser. By default your files are saved in this browser's local storage (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • In a folder you choose. If you pick a local folder, files are read and written there directly.
  • In your own cloud storage, if you connect it. Backup & sync copies your files to your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account. The data goes straight from your browser to Google or Microsoft; it never passes through us.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, Focus Planner asks only for these limited permissions:

  • drive.appdata: a private, hidden app folder in your Drive that only Focus Planner can see. Backups are stored there.
  • drive.file: only files and folders that Focus Planner itself created or that you opened with it. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files.

Sign-in uses Google's OAuth. The access token stays in your browser, is used only to read and write your planner files, and expires after about an hour. We also read your Google account email address from Drive, and keep it only in your browser, so that renewing access picks the right account.

Focus Planner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the planner's storage and sync features to you. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not transferred to anyone else. No human reads it.

Microsoft OneDrive access

If you connect OneDrive, Focus Planner asks only for its own app folder (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder). It cannot access the rest of your OneDrive.

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No cookies of our own. Google and Microsoft set their own cookies on their sign-in pages.

Diagnostics

Settings includes an optional diagnostics recorder. It is off by default and keeps its information in memory in your browser. A diagnostics report is only shared if you copy it and send it yourself.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect a backup in Settings → Backup & Sync. This removes the stored token from your browser.
  • Revoke Focus Planner's Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or its Microsoft access at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Delete the backup copy from Google Drive under Settings → Manage apps in Drive, where you can delete Focus Planner's hidden app data.
  • Clear local data by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
